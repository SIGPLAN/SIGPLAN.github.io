title: "SIGPLAN Diversity Policy"
---

SIGPLAN values diversity in the composition of the organizing and
reviewing committees for its events. There is a broad pool of people
qualified to serve on these committees, but each year organizers must
select only a subset of them. Committee membership is a form of
professional recognition and leadership, so organizers must take care
to select members fairly and to represent the breadth of the relevant
community.

Diversity should be considered both within the current committee and
across successive editions of an event, so that opportunities to serve
are distributed fairly over time.

This policy applies to Program Committees and other reviewing
committees, including External Review Committees. The principles should
also be considered when forming organizing committees. Their
application may be scaled to the size, purpose, and expertise
requirements of each committee.

When forming a reviewing committee, organizers should consider:

1. **Coverage of the event's topics.** The committee should collectively
   have the expertise needed to evaluate submissions across the scope of
   the event.

2. **Rotation and continuity.** Each year's committee should be
   substantially distinct from those of previous years, while allowing
   limited overlap to provide continuity. Members should generally not
   serve on the same conference's Program Committee more often than once
   every three years. Ordinarily, overlap from one year to the next
   should be less than 15%, and no one should serve for more than two
   consecutive years.
   
3. **Institutional diversity.** Ordinarily, no more than two members
   should come from the same institution. For large committees, this
   limit may instead be interpreted as no more than 10% of the committee.
   For the purpose of applying this limit and determining institutional
   conflicts, a large organization with multiple campuses or
   organizational units may be treated as comprising separate
   institutions where those units are comparable in size and
   organizational independence to typical academic institutions.

4. **Type of institution.** The committee should, where relevant,
   include members from academia, industry, government laboratories,
   and other research organizations active in the area.

5. **Career stage and experience.** The committee should include an
   appropriate mix of junior, mid-career, and senior members of the
   community. Conference Program Committee members should normally hold
   a PhD or have comparable research qualifications by the submission
   deadline. For affiliated workshops and similar events, suitably
   qualified senior PhD students may serve where appropriate.

6. **Geographic distribution.** Members should be drawn from the
   regions of the world active in the relevant research area.

7. **Diversity of backgrounds.** The committee should reflect diversity
   in gender and in ethnic, cultural, and other backgrounds relevant to
   representation in the community.

These considerations should be applied holistically rather than as
independent quotas. Organizers should be prepared to explain
significant departures from them, particularly where a committee is
large enough that broader representation could reasonably have been
achieved.

The Program Chair should also avoid selecting a committee that appears
to favor current or former students, postdoctoral researchers,
colleagues, or collaborators. The committee should not be overly
concentrated among frequent collaborators, close colleagues, or
members of the same institution or research group.

Proposed reviewing committees for SIGPLAN-sponsored conferences and 
SIGPLAN-approved workshops must be submitted to the
[SIGPLAN Vice Chair](mailto:vc_sigplan@acm.org?subject=Program%20Committee%20Approval)
for approval by the SIGPLAN Executive Committee, using the
[PC proposal spreadsheet](/Resources/Guidelines/sigplan-pc-proposal-template.xlsx).

Further guidance on committee selection, approval, invitations, and
committee management is available in the
[Guidelines for Program Chairs](/Resources/Guidelines/ProChair/).

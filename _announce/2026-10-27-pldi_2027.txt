PLDI 2027 Call For Papers
========================

*Call for Papers*
====================
2027 ACM Conference on Programming Language Design and Implementation (PLDI)
Sat 5 - Fri 11 June 2027 
Atlanta, Georgia, USA
https://pldi27.sigplan.org/track/pldi-2027-papers

PACMPL Issue PLDI 2027 seeks contributions on all aspects of programming languages research, broadly construed, including design, implementation, theory, applications, and performance.

Authors of papers published in PACMPL Issue PLDI 2027 will be invited – but not required – to present their work in the PLDI conference in June 2027, which is sponsored by ACM SIGPLAN.

*Important Dates*
===============
Thu 12 Nov 2026, Submission Deadline
Tue 16 Feb - Thu 18 Feb 2027, Author Response Period
Thu 4 Mar 2027, Author Notification

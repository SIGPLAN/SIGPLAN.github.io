title: "Tenure-track faculty PL position at Ohio State University"
timestamp: "10/8/2026 15:20:59"
---
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) in the College of Engineering at The Ohio State University invites applications for a tenured/tenure-track faculty position at the Assistant/Associate/Full Professor level to advance innovative research and development in the Programming Languages & Software Engineering (PLSE) areas. The successful candidate for this endowed position will also be named the Timashev (Assistant/Associate/Full) Professor. This position is located on the main Columbus campus of The Ohio State University and starts as early as Fall 2027.

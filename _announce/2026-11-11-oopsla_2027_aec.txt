Dear Colleagues,

We seek nominations for members of the community to serve on the
OOPSLA 2027 Artifact Evaluation Committee (AEC). We encourage
nominations and self-nominations across all levels of seniority, in
particular graduate students and postdocs.

WHAT DOES AEC SERVICE INVOLVE?

In short, AEC members will evaluate artifacts submitted with accepted
OOPSLA 2027 papers. It is the goal of members of the AEC to examine
these artifacts and determine whether they are functional and meet the
expectations laid out in the paper and adequately support its central
claims. We expect reviewers to review 3-5 artifacts.

This involves:

  * Installing, running, and verifying artifacts.
  * Validating artifacts for functionality, reusability and
    reproducibility.
  * Writing reviews and providing recommendations for which badge(s)
    to award.

TIMELINE

OOPSLA's two-round review process includes corresponding artifact
evaluation rounds:

  * Round 1: February-March 2027
  * Round 2: August-September 2027

HOW TO APPLY

Please submit nominations (including self-nominations) via our online
form by 15 December 2026:

  https://forms.gle/Tf8YFiCQ3AcAXy2R9
